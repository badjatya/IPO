## What the company does
Bengaluru-based defence-electronics OEM: thermal imaging cores, weapon sights, thermal binoculars, targeting systems, missile seekers, fire-control and guidance systems; directed-energy products are in development with no revenue yet. Revenue split FY26: tactical systems 58% (₹211.7 Cr), platform systems 40% (₹144.8 Cr). Per Frost & Sullivan (paid for by the company) it is India's largest maker of thermal imaging systems for government/defence by sales value (Apr 2024–Mar 2026) and holds 94% of India's thermal-imaging exports by units. 28,000+ systems deployed in 24 countries; products are ITAR-free and the company owns 100% of its IP (bought from CEAQ Singapore in Mar 2024). Asset-light: design and R&D in-house (Bengaluru, 130 engineers, 339 staff), manufacturing outsourced to EMS partners (Kaynes Technology, Avalon). Revenue FY26: Indian government 71%, private system integrators 24%, foreign government 3%. Order book ₹314.2 Cr (30 Jun 2026; domestic ₹231.9 Cr, international ₹82.4 Cr) plus ₹118.2 Cr of orders received 1 Jul–30 Sep 2026.

## Red flags
- 100% OFS: no money to the company while working capital days have ballooned to 517 and operating cash flow is negative two years running
- FY26 revenue -22.7%, PAT -30%, Q1 FY27 net loss ₹17.7 Cr; RoE dropped from 52.7% to 9.8%
- Lumpy, tender-driven revenue with poor visibility; export share swung from 65.5% to 5.6%; reliance on Indian government (71% of FY26 revenue) and on geopolitical demand spikes
- Inventory up 3x in two years (₹111 Cr → ₹320 Cr); outsourced manufacturing reliance on Kaynes/Avalon
- Bid win ratio declining (14.3% in Q1 FY27); directed-energy business is pre-revenue
- Industry data from a company-paid Frost & Sullivan report; promoters hold only 17.43%; multiple old-parent/investor entities (CEAQ) exiting
- Valuation near 79x trailing earnings that have fallen; litigation: no criminal or regulatory proceedings against the company noted; civil matters not reviewed in full (extract truncated)
- Several RHP details not found in the extract: lower price band, selling-shareholder split, employee discount

## Verdict
**Apply for listing gains only — only if the anchor book is Strong.**
1. Genuine technology franchise (ITAR-free, owned IP, #1 in India thermal imaging) with a ₹314 Cr order book plus ₹118 Cr new orders, which supports an FY27 rebound.
2. GMP ₹345 (~49%) and defence-theme demand favour a listing pop; Retail quota of ≥35% helps allotment odds.
3. But trailing numbers are poor (revenue and profit down, loss-making quarter, negative cash flow, 79x P/E) and the issue is pure OFS, so there is little fundamental cushion if sentiment turns.
Long-term: strong technology in a cyclical, lumpy business at a full price; wait for proof of FY27 execution and working-capital normalisation before holding.
