## What the company does
Goa-based maker of custom polymer compounds (HDPE/MDPE/LLDPE, XLPE, HFFR, cable-filling, adhesive coatings) used in power, telecom and railway cables and 3LPE pipeline coating. 127+ grades, single plant in Goa (installed 35,251 MTPA, ~66% utilised in FY26). Wire & cables 81% of FY26 revenue, industrial pipelines 19%. Products enlisted with CPSEs (a Maharatna power-transmission company, a Navratna engineering consultancy, a Miniratna telecom company).

## Promoters & ownership
