# EAAA India Alternatives (EDELALTS)
_Analysed: 2026-10-22 · Sources: RHP_

## Snapshot
Price band ₹1585–1665 · lot 9 · issue ₹1,200 Cr, 100% OFS by promoter ESIPL (no fresh issue) · 6.47 Cr shares post-issue, market cap ≈ ₹10,765 Cr at upper band · listing 2 Nov 2026.
Quota (RHP Offer Structure): QIB ≤50% · NII ≥15% (sNII 1/3, bNII 2/3) · Retail ≥35%.

## What the company does
Edelweiss group's alternative asset manager: infrastructure, real estate and private credit funds. Earns management, advisory and performance fees plus returns on own fund investments.

## Promoters & ownership
ESIPL holds 89.58% pre-issue; EFSL, EFCL and EGWML are promoters with no direct shares. ESIPL converted CCDs at ₹170/₹173 (face ₹10) in 2024 and sells ₹1,200 Cr.
