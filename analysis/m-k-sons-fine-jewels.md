## What the company does
Retail jewellery chain (gold, diamond, CZ) with 5 showrooms: 3 in Mumbai, 2 in Ahmedabad. Founder Ramchand Raimalani has been in jewellery since 1999; the proprietorship was folded into the company on 29 Mar 2025. Gujarat and Maharashtra showrooms supply ~all revenue (Gujarat share 68–77% in the periods shown). Regional brand, no proven moat.

## Promoters & ownership
Promoters: Ramchand Raimalani (selling shareholder, 34 lakh shares) and Neelam Raimalani. Two bonus issues in FY26 (2:1 in May 2025, 2:3 in Dec 2025) inflated the share count to 4.27 Cr. Post-issue promoter holding: not found.

## Red flags
- Revenue +53% FY26 and gross margin doubling with an unchanged 5-store base; Q1 EBITDA margin 23% is unusually high for jewellery retail.
- Inventory days up to 333 and a net operating cycle of 321 days; more than ₹150 Cr of proceeds go to inventory.
- Promoter is selling; business was only moved into the company in Mar 2025; 2-store-city concentration.
- Small book runner (Aryaman Financial); industry report is company-commissioned.

## Verdict
**Neutral.** Low P/E (13x) and strong growth, but margin jump, bloated inventory and a thin track record as a company. No GMP yet; recheck once GMP and anchors are out.
Long-term view: regional jeweller whose reported margins need to hold before it deserves a peer multiple.
