OVERVIEW
We are an Indian manufacturer and supplier of fire protection equipment and systems, offering a comprehensive portfolio
spanning water, foam, and gas-based fire suppression systems, which are also generally known as fire fighting products. We
develop majority of our products in-house with proprietary designs to meet national and international standards, serving high-
hazard industrial applications as well as residential and commercial sectors. According to the CRISIL Report on page 203, we
were the second-largest manufacturer of fire protection equipment and systems in India in terms of operating income (revenue
from operations) in Fiscal 2025 and the largest exporter by value (equipment) in Fiscal 2025. We have export sales in 49
countries as of the three months period ended June 30, 2026 and more than 90 countries as of Fiscal 2026. With more than three
decades of experience, we have established ourselves as a globally recognized and reliable brand.
In the early 1990s, critical fire protection equipment and systems such as deluge valves, alarm valves, and medium velocity
water spray nozzles were generally imported, with lack of Indian manufacturers offering high-quality alternatives like UL
Listed and FM Approved products (Source: CRISIL Report page 170). Our Company is the first Indian fire protection
equipment and systems manufacturer to receive UL Listing for deluge valves (1996) (Source: CRISIL Report page 202). Since
the commencement of our business, we have consistently focused on providing locally manufactured fire protection equipment
and systems as import-substitutes. We are also the first Indian fire protection equipment and systems manufacturer to receive
FM approval for range of products used in low expansion foam system namely foam concentrates, bladder tanks, proportioners,
topside discharge devices and foam water sprinkler discharge devices (Source: CRISIL Report page 202). Further, we are also
the first Indian company focused on fire protection equipment and systems manufacturing to receive ASME U (pressure vessels)
stamp certification (Source: CRISIL Report page 202). With 21 and 87 UL Listed and FM Approved certifications, as per UL
product certifications and FM product certifications respectively, we have the highest number of UL Listed and FM Approved
product certifications amongst the fire protection equipment focused peers in India as of September 2026 (Source: CRISIL
Report page 202).
Our business was originally carried on under the name M/s HD Fire Protect Co., which was established by Harish Dharamshi
as a partnership firm on April 27, 1990. Our Company was incorporated on April 24, 1997, as ‘D.H. Fire Protect Private
Limited’ and in the year 2005 the name of our Company was changed to ‘HD Fire Protect Private Limited’. Thereafter, on
April 1, 2005, our Company acquired M/s HD Fire Protect Co., as a going concern along with all its assets and liabilities, and
the business was consolidated under a single corporate entity.
As of June 30, 2026, our offerings are spread across 8 product categories, namely - (a) sprinklers, alarm valves and accessories,

COMPETITIVE STRENGTHS
Well established global presence in a certification and stringent empanelment driven, high barrier industry
We have a business legacy of over 36 years, with our fire protection equipment and systems exported to more than 90 countries.
We have developed technical expertise, robust manufacturing capabilities and a global presence in the fire protection equipment
industry, which is characterised by substantial and enduring entry barriers. These entry barriers arise from a combination of
factors, including the requirement for specialised and internationally recognised product certifications (such as UL Listed, FM
Approved, ASME U Stamp, and CE Marking), each of which certifications typically according to the CRISIL Report, on page
194, often require 6-24 months for new product lines. Added to this are stringent regulatory compliance requirements across
jurisdictions, long product development and testing cycles, and complex, capital-intensive manufacturing processes. Sourcing
and procurement processes in B2B such as commercial buildings, industrial spaces, etc. are often influenced by long-standing
vendor relationship and vendor empanelment. It becomes difficult for new entrants to win contracts as established players, who
are already part of approved vendor list, may enjoy the status of preferred vendors (Source: CRISIL Report page 195). Overall,
fire protection is usually a highly regulated industry with long certification cycles, high compliance thresholds, and high-
performance benchmarks. Additionally, several end-use sectors require specialised product approvals, industry-specific
certifications, regular audits, and compliance with geographical certifications, which act as an entry barrier of new players
(Source: CRISIL Report page 195).

Among the fire protection equipment focused peers we are the only company to have all of the following certifications, UL
Solutions, FM Approvals, CE, VdS, ASME-U and Lloyd’s Register, across the following product categories- sprinklers, alarm
valves and accessories, deluge valves, deluge skids and pre-action systems, foam equipment and suppression systems, monitors
and monitor nozzles, systems valves and accessories, water spray nozzles, gas suppression systems (Source: CRISIL Report
page 202). These certifications are essential for participation in regulated and export markets and typically involve multiyear
evaluation cycles comprising product testing, technical documentation, factory inspections and ongoing compliance audits. We
are among the few fire protection equipment and systems companies in India to hold certificate from China National Quality
Testing Center for Fire Fighting Products, (GUANGDONG) for fire protection equipment and system sales in China (Source:

protection, which is renewed from time to time.
Across domestic and international clients, empanelment typically takes 3-12 months depending on review complexity, audit
scheduling, and documentation. Commonly assessed parameters include compliance with technical criteria, testing facilities,
manufacturing and spare capacity, ability to manage concurrent commitments, adherence to safety, health & environment
practices, and robust packaging and logistics standards. Our systematic and well-prepared approach has enabled us to
consistently secure approvals and empanelment.
Our Company is empanelled with an international client through a rigorous multi-step qualification process, and, as per the
CRISIL Report on page 199, our fire protection systems have been supplied at some of the key sites including Saudi Aramco’s
Ras Tanura Sea Island project. This begins with portal registration, submission of statutory documents and certifications,
followed by technical evaluations. Product catalogues are reviewed against client specifications, discussions are held with
technical and sourcing teams across geographies, and a mandatory on-site audit is conducted to assess manufacturing
capabilities, safety, environmental compliance, and alignment with client standards. The process is stringent, expensive, and
time-consuming, often extending over several months to years and requiring significant resource commitments. Upon approval,
our Company is linked to material codes and recognized as a qualified supplier. Once empanelled, customers prefer sourcing
from existing approved vendors for extended periods, making it difficult for new entrants to displace them. This creates a high
entry barrier, further reinforcing the competitive advantage of qualified suppliers like us.

Overview
We are an Indian manufacturer and supplier of fire protection equipment and systems, offering a comprehensive portfolio
spanning water, foam, and gas-based fire suppression systems, which are also generally known as fire fighting products. We
develop majority of our products in-house with proprietary designs to meet national and international standards, serving high-
hazard industrial applications as well as residential and commercial sectors. According to the CRISIL Report on page 203, we
were the second-largest manufacturer of fire protection equipment and systems in India in terms of operating income (revenue
from operations) in Fiscal 2025 and the largest exporter by value (equipment) in Fiscal 2025. We have export sales in 49
countries as of the three months period ended June 30, 2026 and more than 90 countries as of Fiscal 2026. With more than three
decades of experience, we have established ourselves as a globally recognized and reliable brand.
In the early 1990s, critical fire protection equipment and systems such as deluge valves, alarm valves, and medium velocity
water spray nozzles were generally imported, with lack of Indian manufacturers offering high-quality alternatives like UL
Listed and FM Approved products (Source: CRISIL Report page 170). Our Company is the first Indian fire protection
equipment and systems manufacturer to receive UL Listing for deluge valves (1996) (Source: CRISIL Report page 202). Since
the commencement of our business, we have consistently focused on providing locally manufactured fire protection equipment
and systems as import-substitutes. We are also the first Indian fire protection equipment and systems manufacturer to receive
FM approval for range of products used in low expansion foam system namely foam concentrates, bladder tanks, proportioners,
topside discharge devices and foam water sprinkler discharge devices (Source: CRISIL Report page 202). Further, we are also
the first Indian company focused on fire protection equipment and systems manufacturing to receive ASME U (pressure vessels)
stamp certification (Source: CRISIL Report page 202). With 21 and 87 UL Listed and FM Approved certifications, as per UL
  327
product certifications and FM product certifications respectively, we have the highest number of UL Listed and FM Approved
product certifications amongst the fire protection equipment focused peers in India as of September 2026 (Source: CRISIL
Report page 202).
Our business was originally carried on under the name M/s HD Fire Protect Co., which was established by Harish Dharamshi
as a partnership firm on April 27, 1990. Our Company was incorporated on April 24, 1997, as ‘D.H. Fire Protect Private
Limited’ and in the year 2005 the name of our Company was changed to ‘HD Fire Protect Private Limited’. Thereafter, on
April 1, 2005, our Company acquired M/s HD Fire Protect Co., as a going concern along with all its assets and liabilities, and
the business was consolidated under a single corporate entity.
As of June 30, 2026, our offerings are spread across 8 product categories, namely - (a) sprinklers, alarm valves and accessories,
(b) deluge valves, deluge skids and pre-action systems, (c) foam equipment and suppression systems, (d) monitors and monitor
nozzles, (e) systems valves and accessories, (f) water spray nozzles, (g) custom engineered systems, and (h) gas suppression
systems, covering a wide spectrum of (a) industrial end use in sectors such as industrials, heavy engineering, aerospace, oil &
gas, refining and petrochemicals, power & energy, pharmaceuticals, data centres, warehousing amongst others; and (b)
residential and commercial end use in sectors such as hospitality, healthcare, retail, education, banking and warehousing. We
are also empanelled with various customers across sectors where we have presence.
We operate two manufacturing facilities in Maharashtra, Jalgaon MIDC and Thane MIDC, spanning a combined area of 8.50
acres. We have expanded the capacity of our Jalgaon facility, with one unit for manufacturing and another for a dedicated fire
test laboratory, industrial warehouse and R&D centre, admeasuring 2.50 acres, and are also constructing a warehouse at Wagle
Estate, Thane, admeasuring 0.50 acres, which is expected to be completed by November 2026. In addition to our in-house
production, we also undertake contract manufacturing of off-the-shelf products as well as white labelling to cater to diverse
customer requirements. In Fiscal 2026, we supplied our fire protection equipment and systems to 2,066 customers. We supplied
our fire protection equipment and systems across 49 countries in the three-month period ended June 30, 2026, and across more
than 90 countries between inception and March 31, 2026. This international reach is supported by a distributor-led supply chain
for international markets and a stockist model for India.
We maintain a strong focus on quality, reflected in the absence of product recalls and a replacement rate of just 0.01% over the
three months period ended June 30, 2026 and the past three fiscals. Our R&D initiatives are directed toward next-generation
technologies that enhance performance, ensure environmental compliance, and meet global certification standards. We also
specialize in custom-engineered fire protection skids tailored to client-specific requirements and are the only Indian
manufacturer to offer certain advanced systems, such as a next-generation pre-action system. Our innovation pipeline includes
a new deluge valve design, for which a patent application is currently under process. As on the date of this Red Herring
Prospectus, we hold patents in the United States of America, India, Turkey and Saudi Arabia for "pressure operated fluid valve
with configurable diaphragm arrangement", and two design registrations in India for "diaphragm of fire protection valve". We
have also made a patent application in the United Arab Emirates in respect of the same.
We have supplied products at some of the key sites including master stream nozzle installations at India's space launch pad at
Sriharikota, fire protection equipment (alarm valve, monitor, master stream nozzle) at India's largest refinery at Jamnagar, fire
