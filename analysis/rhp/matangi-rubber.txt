Offer for Sale
Our Company will not receive any proceeds from the Offer for Sale. The Selling Shareholders will be entitled to
their respective portion of the Offer Proceeds, to the extent of the Equity Shares offered by them in the Offer, after
deducting their respective proportion of Offer related expenses and relevant taxes thereon, as applicable.
Objects of the Fresh Offer
The Net proceeds of the Fresh Offer i.e. gross proceeds of the Fresh Offer less the Offer expenses apportioned to
our Company (“Net Proceeds”) are proposed to be utilized in the following manner:
1. Repayment/pre-payment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding loans availed by our Company;
2. Funding Capital Expenditure requirements for setting up green field manufacturing facility for Rubber
  Recycling Products (Bhind, Madhya Pradesh);
3. Funding Capital Expenditure requirements for setting up green field manufacturing facility for production of
  Solid Tyres (Bhind, Madhya Pradesh);
4. General Corporate Purposes.
(Collectively referred as the “Objects”)
Further, our Company expects to receive the benefits of listing of the Equity Shares on the Stock Exchanges
including enhancing our visibility and our brand image among our existing and potential customers and creating
a public market for our Equity Shares in India. It will also make future financing easier and affordable in case of
expansion or diversification of the business.
The Objects of the Offer have been approved by our Board pursuant to its resolution dated May 25, 2026.
The main objects and matters in furtherance of the main objects set out in the Memorandum of Association enable
us to undertake its existing activities and the activities for which funds are being raised by us through Fresh Offer.
Further, we confirm that the activities which we have been carrying out till date are in accordance with the object
clause of our Memorandum of Association.

Overview
Our company was incorporated in the year 2004 as “Matangi Rubber Private Limited” pursuant to Certificate of
Incorporation issued by Registrar of Companies, Delhi on June 23, 2004. Further, by passing a special resolution in
the extra ordinary general meeting held on November 04, 2024 the company got converted into a public company and
the name of the company changed from “Matangi Rubber Private Limited” to “Matangi Rubber Limited” via
certificate of incorporation consequent upon conversion to public company issued by the Registrar of Companies,

We are engaged in the business of manufacturing of tyres, tyre-flaps, tubes & rubber compound. Our tyre portfolio
primarily caters to the 2-wheeler and 3-wheeler segments, while the tyre flaps and tubes manufactured by us are mainly
used in commercial vehicles such as trucks, buses, and heavy utility vehicles. Whereas, rubber compound is used in
manufacturing of other rubber materials. We have in total five manufacturing plants out of which two plants are
located at Selaqui (Dehradun, Uttarakhand), two plants at Bhind (Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh) and one plant at
Gummidipoondi (Chennai, Tamil Nadu).
Business Model:
Manufacturing & Selling: A business model wherein a company undertakes the end-to-end manufacturing of products
under its own brand name and subsequently markets and sells such products in the market. In our case, we are engaged
in the manufacturing and selling of tyre tubes under our own brand. Under this arrangement, we procure the required
raw materials independently and carry out the complete manufacturing process using our in-house production
facilities. The finished products are thereafter marketed, distributed, and sold by us through our sales network to
customers across various markets.
  Page | 202
Contract Manufacturing: Contract manufacturing also referred to as outsourcing or third-party manufacturing is a
business arrangement in which one company (the client) engages another company (the contract manufacturer) to
produce goods on its behalf. As a contract manufacturer, we specialize in the production of tyre flaps. Utilizing raw
materials procured specifically for this purpose and employing our in-house manufacturing capabilities, we produce
tyre flaps customized with the client’s logo. These finished products are then sold to the respective customers.
Job Work Service: A business model in which a principal company outsources a specific segment of its manufacturing
process to an external manufacturing company. In our case, we provide manufacturing services for tyres, tyre flaps &
tubes to principal. Under this arrangement, we perform the designated processing or conversion work on behalf of the
principal company. Wherein, the raw materials are provided by the principal company and we are only responsible
for converting such raw material into tyres, tyre-flaps or tubes, using our manufacturing facilities for which we receive
conversion charges based on the agreed terms from the principal company.

I. Selaqui (Dehradun, Uttarakhand) – Tyre Flap & Rubber Compound Plant
  Since 2007, we are engaged into contract manufacturing of tyre flaps at Selaqui (Dehradun, Uttarakhand) facility
  for one of our primary customer i.e. JK Tyre and other customers in the industry. As a contract manufacturer, we
  specialize in the production of tyre flaps, utilizing raw materials procured specifically for this purpose and
  employing our in-house manufacturing capabilities, we produce tyre flaps customized with the client’s logo. These
  finished products are then sold to the respective customers. We are also engaged into manufacturing & selling of
  Rubber Compound to small extent to customers other than JK Tyre.
  Process for Contract Manufacturing of Tyre Flaps
  Rubber flaps are essential components used in commercial and heavy-duty vehicles, primarily placed between the
  tyre and the inner tube. The manufacturing of rubber flaps involves several precise steps, ensuring durability,
  flexibility, and resistance to wear and tear.
 a) Raw Material Selection and Preparation
  The manufacturing process of rubber flaps begins with the careful selection & procurement of raw materials.
  These flaps are produced using a blend of natural rubber (NR) and synthetic rubbers such as Styrene-Butadiene
  Rubber (SBR) or Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer (EPDM), chosen based on the desired performance
  characteristics. These components are accurately weighed in accordance with precise formulations and prepared
  for the subsequent mixing stage.
 b) Mixing to prepare “Master Compound”.
  Rubber compounding is performed using a Banbury mixer (Inter Mix Machine), where raw materials as prepared
  in the first step are thoroughly blended under controlled temperature and pressure conditions to produce a uniform
  compound. Ensuring proper dispersion of these raw materials is critical for maintaining consistent product quality.
  Page | 207
c)  Sheet Formation (Calendaring or Extrusion)
  Following the mixing process, the rubber master compound is processed through Calendaring (Mixing Mill or
  Extrusion) machines to form continuous sheets. This process is carried out to further ensure proper blending of
  raw materials. After calendaring, the sheets are cut out in a tile shape after it is cooled down through cooling
  process. Post this, the master compound undergoes testing to evaluate its rheological and mechanical properties,
  confirming that it meets the customer specifications.
d) Mixing to prepare “Final Compound”.
  The master compound in tile shaped form is then feed in a kneader along with various fillers and additives such
  as carbon black, processing oils and accelerators. Such additional raw materials are added to improve strength,
  flexibility and processing efficiency of the rubber. The rubber compound prepared under this stage is the Final
  Compound.
e)  Sheet Formation (Calendaring or Extrusion)
  Following the mixing process, the rubber final compound is processed through calendaring (Mixing Mill or
  Extrusion) machines to form continuous sheets of a specified thickness. The thickness and width of each sheet
  are determined by the flap design, which varies depending on the size and type of tyre for which the flaps are
  made. This process is carried out to further ensure proper blending of raw materials used to prepare final
  compound. After calendaring, the sheets are feed to a conveyor belt which goes through a cooling process.
f)  Cutting and Preforming
  These rubber sheets are then carried through the conveyor belt, wherein they are cut manually by workers, into
  the required dimensions based on the design specifications of the flaps.
g) Molding and Curing (Vulcanization)
  The cut-outs of rubber sheets are placed into compression moulds or hydraulic press, which are preheated to
  specific curing temperatures, typically ranging from 150°C to 170°C. The vulcanization process is then carried
  out under controlled heat and pressure for a defined duration, enabling the rubber to undergo crosslinking and
  develop its final physical properties, such as elasticity, strength, and heat resistance. During this stage, the mold
  also imparts the final shape, dimensions, and any required branding or surface markings onto the flap.
h) Trimming and Finishing
  Once cured, the flaps are removed from the mould and cooled. Excess rubber from the edges is trimmed manually
  to ensure a smooth and uniform finish.
i)  Quality Inspection and Testing
  Each batch of rubber flaps undergoes quality control checks. Tests include visual inspection, dimensional checks,
  tensile strength, elongation and flexibility. Flaps are also tested for heat resistance and durability under simulated
  conditions. Further, a hole is punched for valve in the final tyre flaps, before being packed for final delivery.
j)  Packaging and Dispatch
  The flaps are then packed in bundles or boxes based on customer requirements. Proper labeling is done before
  dispatching to the respective customers. The finished goods (tyre flaps) are either transported by our owned
  vehicles or it is picked up by the respective customers from the manufacturing plant.

Process of Manufacturing & Selling Rubber Compound
a) Raw Material Procurement & Preparation
  The manufacturing process of rubber compound begins with the careful selection & procurement of raw materials.
  These rubber compounds are produced using a blend of natural rubber (NR) and synthetic rubbers such as Styrene-
