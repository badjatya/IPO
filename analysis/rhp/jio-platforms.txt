progressive deleveraging of the balance sheet, further strengthened by the proposed prepayment from the Net
Proceeds, will position the Company favourably for continued investment in its strategic priorities, including 5G
network densification and expansion, fixed broadband penetration, AI and cloud services, enterprise digital
services, and international technology partnerships, while maintaining the financial flexibility to pursue business
development opportunities as they arise.

Overview
We are a technology platform, built on proprietary digital technology and pan-India digital connectivity as the
foundational layer, driving digital transformation in India. Since our inception, we have played a pivotal role in
democratising access to digital connectivity and digital services in India. Jio Platforms’ digital connectivity forms
one of the fundamental pillars of India’s growing digital economy, which is expected to reach $1.4 trillion (₹125.8
trillion) by Fiscal 2031, as per the Analysys Mason Report. Our Subsidiary, Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited
(“RJIL”), served 533.3 million customers in India, as of June 30, 2026. A combination of mobile, fixed broadband
and digital services across a unified platform, together with data and customer personalisation, positions us as a
comprehensive digital gateway for accessing the internet, digital services and emerging technologies, including
artificial intelligence (“AI”).

capabilities, from operating systems to digital applications. Technology is the fundamental architecture of how
our business is structured, built, and operated.

platform is built on five integrated layers: (i) Digital products and services – our consumer applications and
enterprise solutions, (ii) Digital connectivity – including our in-house developed network core, platforms and
devices; (iii) Phygital access at scale – our phygital distribution network; (iv) Deep customer insights – the
customer data and analytics we process; and (v) Proprietary technology – comprising our intellectual property
portfolio and licensed trademarks. As a result, we are able to deliver transformative 71 digital connectivity at

Overview
We are a technology platform, built on proprietary digital technology and pan-India digital connectivity as the
foundational layer, driving digital transformation in India. Since our inception, we have played a pivotal role in
democratising access to digital connectivity and digital services in India. Jio Platforms’ digital connectivity forms
one of the fundamental pillars of India’s growing digital economy, which is expected to reach $1.4 trillion (₹125.8
trillion) by Fiscal 2031, as per the Analysys Mason Report. Our Subsidiary, Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited
(“RJIL”), served 533.3 million customers in India, as of June 30, 2026. A combination of mobile, fixed broadband
and digital services across a unified platform, together with data and customer personalisation, positions us as a
comprehensive digital gateway for accessing the internet, digital services and emerging technologies, including
artificial intelligence (“AI”).
Our platform is built on five integrated layers (i) Digital products and services – our consumer applications and
enterprise solutions, (ii) Digital connectivity – including our in-house developed network core, platforms and
  436
devices; (iii) Phygital86 access at scale – our phygital distribution network; (iv) Deep customer insights – the
customer data and analytics we process; and (v) Proprietary technology – comprising our intellectual property
portfolio and licensed trademarks. Our digital products and services are delivered on our platform services layer,
which provides a single unified interface for consumers and businesses to access connectivity, compute, content,
undertake financial services and utilise AI capabilities. Our digital connectivity, which forms the foundation layer,
delivers pan-India reach to over 99% of the population for wireless broadband as of June 30, 2026. Building on
this connectivity, our phygital architecture enables us to rapidly design, deploy and scale new digital services
while maintaining operational efficiency and consistent customer experience across all touchpoints. As a result,
our large and highly engaged customer base generates more data, which we can then leverage to improve our
platforms and enhance customer experience and satisfaction. Underpinning this ecosystem, our proprietary
technology capabilities are deployed across network, device engineering, software and operating systems and
digital applications, collectively powering our digital connectivity business.
Our financial performance should be evaluated in the context of our positioning as a technology platform. Our
platform creates multiple monetisation layers from our portfolio of offerings across digital connectivity and digital
services, including entertainment, cloud, managed services, IoT 87 and CPaaS. This multi-layered revenue
architecture, combined with our proprietary technology stack and vertically integrated cost structure, underpins
our EBITDA margins.
