## What the company does
Delhi-based agrochemicals and hybrid seeds maker (founded 1994). FY26: crop protection 80.4% of operating revenue (₹2,476.57 Cr), seeds 19.0% (₹585.19 Cr). 4 formulation units, 2 technical plants, 3 seed units; 14,717 distributors, 34 distribution centres, 23 states. 28 patent applications (18 granted in India); top-three in pearl millet and mustard seed.

## Red flags

ROE 6-8% against ~24x P/E. Weak Q1 cash conversion, heavy working capital; 22% OFS; pre-IPO struck at the upper band; industry report company-commissioned.

## Verdict
**Neutral.** Growth and margin expansion are real (EBITDA 9.4% → 14.5%), proceeds mostly cut debt. But pricing is full on returns, the CBI/ED case is unresolved and Q1 cash flow is weak. Revisit after anchors and GMP. Long-term: decent diversified agri-inputs business; returns must improve to justify ~24x.
