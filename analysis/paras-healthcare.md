## What the company does
Operates 8 "Paras Health" tertiary/quaternary hospitals with 2,211 beds (Gurugram, Panchkula, Patna, Darbhanga, Kanpur, Udaipur, Ranchi, Srinagar) across North India, Bihar, Jharkhand and J&K. CONGOR specialties are ~74% of revenue. 1,084 doctors, 1,937 nurses; mix of owned and leased hospitals.

## Promoters & ownership
Promoter Dr. Dharminder Kumar Nagar (MD) sells up to ₹300 Cr; PE investor Commelina Ltd sells up to ₹650 Cr. 360 ONE and Axis AIF came in at ₹237/share in Apr 2025 (offer at ₹660–693 is ~2.8–2.9x that). Web sources put promoter holding at ~72.8% pre-issue (not verified in RHP extract).
