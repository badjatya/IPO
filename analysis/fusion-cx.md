## What the company does
Customer-experience (CX) outsourcer: voice, email, chat, social and messaging support, plus AI data services (data collection, annotation, teleoperations). 40 delivery centres and 2 sales offices in 13 countries. Verticals: telecom and utilities 47% of FY26 revenue (31% in FY24), plus high-tech/travel, BFSI, retail, healthcare. Part of its growth is acquisition-led (goodwill ₹288 Cr in FY25 to ₹362 Cr in FY26). Industry data is from a company-commissioned Everest report.

## Promoters & ownership
Promoters Pankaj Dhanuka (Chairman, MD & CEO) and Kishore Saraogi (COO & MD), both US-resident, via P N S Business Pvt Ltd and Rasish Consultants Pvt Ltd. Promoters hold ≈96.2% pre-issue, so there is no PE or institutional holder to exit. The OFS (₹202 Cr) is by the two promoter entities.

## Valuation vs peers
FY26 basic EPS ₹13.47 gives **P/E ≈21.5x at the ₹289 cap on pre-issue earnings** (snapshot shows 21.62). Peers (RHP): FirstSource 22.3x, eClerx 19.7x, Alldigi 14.1x, Inventurus 31.4x, Sagility 21.7x, Hinduja Global 51.1x; average 26.7x. **Fair** vs peers: not cheap given 96% promoter holding, acquisition-led growth and a one-year jump in margins.

## Use of proceeds
Fresh ₹500 Cr: ₹275.7 Cr repay or prepay borrowings of the company and subsidiaries (all in FY27); ₹61.1 Cr investment in step-down subsidiaries Omind Technologies for the Arya and MindVoice IT tools; balance for unidentified acquisitions and general corporate purposes (capped at 35% of gross proceeds, and each of the two at no more than 25%). OFS ₹202 Cr goes to the promoters.

## Red flags
- Top-10 customers were 49.8% / 52.2% / 45.3% of revenue (latest period, FY26, FY25 per RHP risk factor), and telecom is now 47% of revenue.
- Acquisition-led growth: goodwill is ₹363 Cr against net worth ₹620 Cr, and part of the proceeds goes to unidentified acquisitions.
- Promoters sell ₹202 Cr in the OFS; they are US-resident and hold 96% of the company.
- Margin jump in FY26 (PAT 5.6% to 9.3%): sustainability is unproven. Industry report is company-commissioned.

## Verdict
**Neutral / Apply for listing gains only if GMP holds.** Reasons: 21.5x P/E is in line with peers on fast growth (37% revenue, 2.3x PAT); GMP is modest (₹10 on the snapshot, 3.5%; web trackers vary widely, so treat it as unreliable); the retail quota is only 10% and 75% is QIB, so the issue depends on institutional demand. Long-term view: a decent, cash-generative BPO/CX business, but customer concentration and acquisition dependence cap the quality.
