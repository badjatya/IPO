## What the company does
Technology-led "manufacturing platform": aggregates 6,979 third-party suppliers in 26 countries plus 26 own plants, coordinated by Zetwerk OS. Segments: Manufacturing (energy products ₹6,508 Cr, precision ₹1,397 Cr, capital goods ₹1,464 Cr in FY26) and an Ecosystem business (Terra91, raw-material aggregation). 83% of revenue from India. RHP says no listed peer has a similar model.
