# Jio Platforms (JIOPLATFORMS)
_Analysed: 2026-10-15 · Sources: RHP (price band/peer section not extracted)_

## Snapshot
Upper band ₹1,119 · lot 12 · issue ₹30,213 Cr (snapshot) · 100% fresh issue of up to 27 Cr shares (RHP) · ~₹10.3 lakh Cr market cap at upper band (8,939 Cr shares pre-issue + 27 Cr) · open 21 Oct, listing 28 Oct.
Quota (RHP): QIB not more than 50% · NII not less than 15% · **Retail not less than 35%**; Employee reservation up to ₹500 Cr; RIL shareholder reservation up to ₹2,500 Cr.

## What the company does
Reliance's digital/telecom holding company. Subsidiary RJIL served 533.3 million customers at 30 Jun 2026; JioAirFiber 14.0 million (India's largest FWA base). Proprietary 5G SA stack, 7,220 patent applications, pivoting to AI/cloud/enterprise services. RJIL is ~88% of revenue.

## Promoters & ownership
Promoter: Reliance Industries Ltd. Existing investors since 2020: Meta, Google, PIF, Silver Lake, KKR, ADIA, Mubadala, General Atlantic, TPG, Vista, Intel, Qualcomm, L Catterton. This is a pure fresh issue, so no OFS sellers.

**Strong** — ₹8,110 Cr raised (7.25 crore shares at ₹1,119) on 19 Oct 2026. Top names: LIC ₹499 Cr (6.2%), New World Fund (Capital Group) ₹487 Cr (6.0%), BNP Paribas ODI ₹303 Cr (3.7%), EuPac Fund ₹156 Cr, Fidelity funds (Contrafund ₹123 Cr, Blue Chip Growth ₹71 Cr and others), BlackRock Global Funds (multiple, ~₹37 Cr top), American Funds/Capital Group trusts. Lock-in: 50% 30 days, 50% 90 days.

## Red flags
Huge size (₹30,213 Cr) and ₹10 lakh Cr valuation leave little room; growth mid-teens; heavy spectrum-related liabilities and rising finance costs; industry data from a company-commissioned report; 100% proceeds go to debt repayment, not growth.

## Verdict
**Neutral** — best-in-class cash generation and a likely strong listing premium on the GMP, but valuation is full and the size caps upside. Retail quota of 35% makes a one-lot allotment plausible; recheck after subscription.
Long-term view: quality compounder, fairly priced.
